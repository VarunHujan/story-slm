Captain Oliver stood in the narrow, dark water of the Marco Station, watching his ship drift steadily toward a rocky island where he had been sailing for years before, no longer terrified by the danger lurking beneath its surface. Inside the ship's small wooden cabin, Captain Oliver noticed something far more frightening than any fish or car could ever bring him back to shore on such an ordinary day. He blinked, replaced by a small, familiar smell from the old, unfamiliar place in the ship's cool blue hull that seemed almost too frightening to believe. The ship's crew had been planning this mission carefully all season long, but for now, they had decided that staying safe was the most important thing they should do every single night. Captain Oliver let himself think hard, remembering that next week, his crew would be forced aboard the ship, and it might not go exactly as planned either. He looked up at the map again, realizing that the ocean itself felt different, and the storm outside seemed to grow closer with each passing hour until the ship's stern finally snapped into silence, a sinking feeling of dread hanging over him in his chest. His heart raced as he realized the captain's fear was real, a feeling that he knew was far worse than he had imagined possible. As the hours ticked by, a wave of panic swept through the deck faster than any ship above them, making Oliver feel more frightened than worried. A sudden gust of wind sent the ship tumbling off course, crashing against the deck followed immediately by another group of ships rushing past quickly before it even touched the rocky island ahead. Captain Oliver struggled desperately throughout his first few minutes as the waves reached his knees while the ship's crew waited patiently below just to check if the storm was still strong enough to keep them going. After what felt like hours passed without any sign of the storm, the captain called for backup, desperate to see where their crew were supposed to go. Oliver checked the ship clock the moment it came through the clouds: the storm passed steadily, not completely overwhelming, but almost steady and steady despite the odds stacked against him. That's when the captain spotted a large patch of seaweed near the base of their ship, growing larger with every passing second when the storm continued.
